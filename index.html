About Our Library & Reading Ministry

At Crosspoint Bible Church, we believe that serious reading fuels deep worship. Our library is curated to help believers in Kiamunyi, Nakuru, and beyond grow deeper in biblical truth, historical theology, and Christian living. Whether you are looking to borrow a volume for personal study or read literature onsite in our study corner, these resources are made available to strengthen your walk with Christ.
